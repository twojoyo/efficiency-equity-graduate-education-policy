# Efficiency and Equity: Changing Attention in China's Graduate Education Policy, 1987–2022

This repository contains the raw corpus used in the paper:

**Efficiency and Equity: Changing Attention in China's Graduate Education Policy, 1987–2022**

## Data file

- `原始数据：教育部历年工作要点.zip` — archived HTML/DOC files of the annual Work Points of China's Ministry of Education, 1987–2022.

## Corpus notes

- The corpus covers 35 usable years between 1987 and 2022.
- The 2020 Work Points are not included because the full document was not available.
- The 1998 text was supplemented from an authoritative source.
- The documents are publicly available government documents from the Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of China.
